Common Questions

Things people ask
before they book.

01 How long does the final gallery take to deliver?

+

Our standard turnaround time is 3-4 weeks for portrait and corporate sessions, and 6-8 weeks for full weddings. We take our time to ensure every image is meticulously curated and perfectly graded.

02 Do you travel outside of your primary cities?

+

Yes. While we are based across three main cities, we regularly accept commissions globally for editorial, documentary, and destination wedding projects. Travel expenses are calculated transparently during the quote process.

03 Do you offer both digital and physical albums?

+

Absolutely. Every project includes a secure, high-resolution digital gallery. For weddings and major milestones, we highly encourage adding a handcrafted physical album, which serves as a timeless family heirloom.

04 Can we give you a shot list?

+

We absolutely welcome a brief list of must-have family groupings or specific branding requirements. However, we ask that you trust our creative documentary approach for the majority of the day to ensure genuine, candid moments.
