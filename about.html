About Stackly

Eleven photographers. Three cities. One process, followed exactly.

We started in a spare bedroom with one camera and a stack of borrowed film. A decade later, that's still the whole idea.

Our Story

Fewer, better frames instead of endless ones — that's still the whole idea.

Stackly was founded in 2016 by two photographers tired of templated weddings: five hundred nearly-identical frames, dumped on a drive, never printed. Film costs something, so every frame has to earn its place.

Today we are eleven photographers in Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Goa. Every shoot is planned, shot, and edited by hand. Film first, digital backup, a printed album at the end — not just a download link.

What we believe

Four rules. No exceptions.

The same discipline on a wedding day, an office function, or a dawn hike.

01

Shoot with limits

A single roll of 36 makes every frame count. We maintain this strict photographic discipline even when shooting digital formats.

02

Do it by hand

Every single roll of film is developed, processed, and scanned in-house. We do not trust external labs with your creative memories.

03

Deliver a print

A digital link is not a finished deliverable. Every one of our projects ends with a handcrafted physical print you can hold.

04

Stay small

We deliberately choose to turn down extra work rather than shooting past what our small team of eleven can document with care.
